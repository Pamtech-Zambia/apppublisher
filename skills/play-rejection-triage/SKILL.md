# Play Rejection Triage

## Workflow
1. Capture the exact notice, policy name, version/track, date and deadline.
2. Verify the named policy live on official Google sources.
3. Identify the actual offending code, permission, SDK, listing claim, image, declaration or reviewer-access problem.
4. Separate confirmed cause from hypotheses.
5. Fix the underlying behavior first; then make declarations/listing match reality.
6. Validate the corrected build/listing.
7. Appeal only with factual evidence when compliance is real.

## Never
Do not hide functionality, create deceptive reviewer behavior, provide fake credentials, change declarations merely to get past review, or recommend misleading screenshots/listing text.
